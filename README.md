# LUMÉRA Atelier | Luxury Botanical & Clinical Beauty

An e-commerce and interactive consultation platform for **LUMÉRA Atelier**, a clean beauty house pairing clinical actives with pure botanicals.

Built with **React 19**, **TypeScript**, **Tailwind CSS v4**, and modern UX design principles.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Botanical & Clinical Product Catalog**
  - High-performance skincare, complexion tints, lipid elixirs, and treatment balms.
  - Multi-criteria filtering by category, skin concern, formulation finish, and price.
  - Real-time instant search with highlighted query matches.
  - Multi-currency switcher (USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, JPY).

- **Personalized AI Skin Diagnostic & Beauty Concierge**
  - 4-step diagnostic evaluating skin type, primary concerns, and ritual depth.
  - Tailored AM & PM protocols with clinical active synergy recommendations.
  - One-click bundle ordering with integrated 15% complete ritual savings.
  - Interactive AI Concierge to query ingredient layering, routine advice, and formula compatibility.

- **Precision Shade Match Diagnostic**
  - Interactive shade selector matching skin tone depth (Fair, Light, Medium, Deep) and undertone (Cool, Neutral, Warm, Olive).
  - High-resolution swatch previews with instant bag addition.

- **Comprehensive Product Atelier Modals**
  - Detailed product views with multi-angle imagery, clinical study metrics, and third-party validation.
  - Tabbed deep-dives into Key Actives, full INCI ingredient transparency, and application rituals.
  - Community reviews with verified buyer badges, ratings, and upvoting.

- **Flexible Purchasing Options**
  - One-time delivery or auto-replenish subscriptions with 10% discount and customizable frequencies (30, 60, or 90 days).

- **Seamless Shopping Experience**
  - Dynamic Slide-Over Cart Atelier with real-time free shipping threshold progress bar.
  - Complimentary deluxe samples selection for qualifying orders.
  - Wishlist drawer with one-click transfer to cart.
  - Multi-step express checkout modal with gift notes and clean standard certifications.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Framework**: [React 19](https://react.dev/) + [Vite](https://vitejs.dev/)
- **Language**: [TypeScript](https://www.typescriptlang.org/)
- **Styling**: [Tailwind CSS v4](https://tailwindcss.com/)
- **Animations**: [Motion](https://motion.dev/)
- **Icons**: [Lucide React](https://lucide.dev/)
- **Typography**: Cormorant Garamond & Plus Jakarta Sans
